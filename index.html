Our Story

10 Years of Love and Adventure

2016

The Beginning

In São Paulo, two paths crossed and everything changed. We knew right away this was something special - moving in together and taking our first adventure to Chile. Little did we know this was just the beginning of a decade-long journey.

2017

A Bold Move

A life-changing job offer from Facebook meant moving to another hemisphere. We knew the distance might test our relationship, but we believed in what we had - and took the leap together.

2018

Getting Married

The year everything became official. A surprise proposal (watch the video!), and then Roberta took the bravest leap - leaving her career, her family, and everything familiar to start over in a new country without knowing the language. Some things are worth the risk.

2019

Exploring the World

Without a traditional wedding celebration, we decided to create our own memories - a romantic photo shoot in Paris. Our "honeymoon" took us through Venice and Rome, and somehow we managed to explore 7 countries that year.

2020

Putting Down Roots

We bought our first house and discovered we were expecting our first baby.

2021

Welcome Levy

Our greatest adventure yet - becoming parents. Levy arrived and changed everything, filling our home with joy, laughter, and a whole new kind of love.

2023

Welcome Leo

Just when we thought our hearts were full, Leo came along and proved there's always room for more love. Our little family was now complete - two boys, endless energy, and twice the joy (and trouble 😅)

2026

Celebrating 10 Years

Ten years of love, adventures, trips, and two amazing boys - time to celebrate with everyone we love. We hope to see you there!
